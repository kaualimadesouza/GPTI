# Scrum (Scrum Guide 2020)

Source of truth: the Scrum Guide 2020 (scrumguides.org). The group's slides and research guide are
in `seminario-scrum/`; reuse their wording and their MarmitaFit example.

Scrum rests on **transparência, inspeção e adaptação**. The values are commitment, focus,
openness, respect and courage. The Scrum Team is small (typically 10 people or fewer) and has no
sub-teams or hierarchy. Its accountabilities are the **Product Owner** (maximises value and
orders the Product Backlog), the **Scrum Master** (the team's effectiveness) and the **Developers**
(the plan and the Increment).

## Events (timeboxes for a one-month Sprint)

| Evento | Timebox | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint | 1 month or less, fixed | Container of all other events; no changes that endanger the Sprint Goal, and quality does not drop |
| Sprint Planning | up to 8 h | Why the Sprint is valuable (Sprint Goal), what can be done, how |
| Daily Scrum | 15 min | Developers inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the plan |
| Sprint Review | up to 4 h | Scrum Team and stakeholders inspect the outcome and adapt the Product Backlog; a working session, not a demo |
| Sprint Retrospective | up to 3 h | Scrum Team inspects how it works and picks improvements |

Shorter Sprints get proportionally shorter timeboxes, except the Daily, which stays at 15 minutes.

## Artifacts and commitments

Each artifact contains one commitment:

| Artefato | Compromisso | Without it (group's slide 6) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product Backlog: emergent, ordered list of what the product needs | **Product Goal**: the long-term objective; one at a time | Demands with no common direction |
| Sprint Backlog: Sprint Goal + selected items + the plan, by and for the Developers | **Sprint Goal**: the single objective of the Sprint | Tasks with no cohesion |
| Increment: a usable step toward the Product Goal; several can happen in one Sprint | **Definition of Done**: the quality bar | Incomplete is not done |

Work that does not meet the Definition of Done is not part of an Increment and cannot be
presented at the Review.

## Anti-patterns (group's slide 9)

- **Sem foco:** a Sprint full of tasks with no common goal. Ask what outcome the team is trying
  to produce.
- **Sem transparência:** incomplete work presented as done. Ask whether the Increment meets the DoD.
- **Sem adaptação:** events happen, but nothing changes. Ask what changed because of the inspection.

## Pitfalls when writing about Scrum

- The **Definition of Ready (DoR)** is a common team practice, not part of the Scrum Guide. The
  MarmitaFit retro uses it, so present it as a complement.
- The Guide calls them "accountabilities", not "papéis": the 2020 edition dropped "roles".
- The Daily is not a status report to a manager, and the PO does not hand over ready tasks at
  Planning (group's slide 4).
